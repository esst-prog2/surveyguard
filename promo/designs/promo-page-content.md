## Problem Statement
The SurveyGuard promo page (live at https://esst-prog2.github.io/surveyguard/, source `docs/index.html`) already says what the tool is for, who it is for, and how to try it. It does not answer the question its first readers will ask: "why this, and not an existing tool?" The first readers are the course teacher and classmates (user's choice, Q1).

## What Makes This Cool
SurveyGuard turns three separate warning signs into one explained verdict. Here a "flag" means a triggered detector (straightlining, logical contradiction, Mahalanobis outlier), not a flag reason: straightlining on two scales is still one triggered detector, and with the ESS configs' `mahalanobis_min_scales` the outlier detector triggers only on outliers across several scales. A single triggered detector is only a warning: with the ESS rule files (`config/ess1.json` to `config/ess11.json`) and `config/scale_logic.json` (equal weights 1.0, `flag_threshold` 50) and all three detectors available, one triggered detector gives a quality score of 66.7 (not suspicious) and two give 33.3 (suspicious). `config/spike_logic.json` is excluded from this claim: it sets the contradiction weight to 0.0, so only two detectors count there. Every flag carries its reason: the scale, the rule, the respondent's actual values. That matches the literature's advice to combine indicators instead of trusting one screen.
